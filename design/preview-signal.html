ResearchFrom individual movement to species ranges

Animal movement in dynamic landscapes

Ring recoveries record where a bird was marked and where it was found again — millions of paired points across Europe. I use them to estimate dispersal kernels and ask what the long tail means for connectivity and range limits.

  • EURING recoveries
  • Dispersal kernels
  • Movement ecology

Biodiversity monitoring and modelling

Distribution models that ignore dispersal, population dynamics and imperfect detection answer the wrong question confidently. I build models that carry those processes, and integrate data sources never designed to be combined.

  • eBird
  • Occupancy models
  • Camera traps
  • AudioMoth

Conservation under global change

Which species are most exposed, why, and where problems surface first — from large carnivores recolonising Iberia to mammal declines in the Gran Chaco.

  • Landscape permeability
  • Range forecasts
  • Threat mapping

ProjectsFunded work in progress

INTRADISP · 2024–26

Dispersal differences within species

Synthesising intraspecific variation in dispersal across European birds, to test how much of the variation we average away actually matters for range forecasts.

PI · UCM · with Potsdam & BTO

RIMed-Fauna · 2024–27

Wildlife in rivers that run dry

Camera trapping and distribution modelling across the National Parks network, tracking how vertebrates cope with Mediterranean rivers that flood and then disappear.

Team · PI M. M. Sánchez-Montoya

SHAREPOINT · 2025–27

Social structure and parasite sharing

Understory birds in French Guiana: how social organisation shapes who shares parasites with whom, combining networks with disease ecology.

Team · PI J. Pérez-Tris · CEBA
